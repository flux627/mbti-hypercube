HDR headroom test

A WebGPU canvas configured with rgba16float + extended tone mapping. The left column is SDR white — the brightest a normal page element gets. If your browser and display support HDR, the 2× and 4× rows glow brighter than that white, and the bottom row pulses between SDR and 4×. If every row looks the same, something in the chain is clamping to SDR (see status below).

The bottom ramp measures your headroom: white brightens continuously from 1× (left) to 4× (right), with dark ticks at 2× and 3×. The point where it stops getting brighter is the display's current EDR headroom. It moves with screen brightness — lower the brightness and the flat part should shrink; at max brightness there is usually no headroom at all.
